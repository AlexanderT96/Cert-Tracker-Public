## Scope rules

Each entry covers originals, sequels, prequels, meaningful spin-offs, remakes/remasters/definitive editions, expansions/substantial DLC, major live-service phases, platform releases, reboots/revivals, successor projects, licensed adaptations, demos/betas/playtests/early access, major showcases, delays/cancellations, major reveals/trailers and newly announced projects.

### Special mappings

- Warhammer covers major Fantasy, Old World, Age of Sigmar, 40K, Horus Heresy, Necromunda, Blood Bowl, Quest, Dawn of War, Vermintide, Darktide, Space Marine, Rogue Trader, Mechanicus and Chaos Gate lines. LOTR remains separate.
- Monster Hunter includes Stories and meaningful spin-offs.
- Tom Clancy covers branded Ghost Recon, Rainbow Six, The Division, Splinter Cell and related branded series.
- Star Wars covers major licensed games including KOTOR, KOTOR II, the remake, TOR-era projects and Galactic Battlegrounds.
- Total War covers historical, fantasy and Saga lines; Total War: WARHAMMER maps primarily to Total War and secondarily to Warhammer.
- Remedy Connected Universe covers Alan Wake, Control and confirmed connected-universe projects. Max Payne remains separate.
- Persona / Shin Megami Tensei does not mean all Atlus games.
- ARK and ATLAS remain separate.
- Baldur’s Gate maps primarily to Baldur’s Gate; broader D&D coverage includes Neverwinter Nights, Icewind Dale, Planescape, Neverwinter and Dark Alliance.
- Eugen Systems umbrella includes Wargame, WARNO and Steel Division.
- Paradox grand-strategy umbrella includes Stellaris, Crusader Kings, Europa Universalis, Hearts of Iron, Victoria, Age of Wonders and directly related lines, not every Paradox-published game.
- Titanfall excludes Apex Legends.
- Marvel yields to dedicated rows when a covered franchise has its own entry.
- Valve umbrella covers Half-Life, Portal and Left 4 Dead.
- Eora includes Pillars of Eternity, Pillars II and Avowed.
- World of Darkness includes major official game lines.
- Prey covers the 2006 and 2017 games without claiming continuity.
- Sniper Elite excludes Zombie Army.
- MechWarrior / BattleTech is a shared umbrella.
- Path of Exile includes PoE, PoE2 and substantial leagues/seasons/expansions/endgame/classes/platforms/betas/EA/major GGG changes.
- Track major new FromSoftware IPs as discovery candidates before mapping.
- Exclude Apex Legends, Minecraft, Factorio, Satisfactory and RimWorld unless explicitly added later.
